# v0.9.0 audit review (v1.0 gate, #215)

Audited tag: `v0.9.0` (`a0d3894`), frozen read-only checkout.

There were two independent passes, one by Opus and one by Grok. Their findings were merged and deduplicated here, and the disputed ones were re-checked against the code.

**Result:** no Critical or High findings after review. There are 8 Medium findings, and the rest are Low or Info. They are filed as 9 batched issues in milestone v1.0 (#486-#494).

## Review decisions
- **G-01 (Grok: High) became SEC-03 (Medium).** Downgraded and merged.
  - The speaker never listens: `internal/rib/rib.go:312-314` maps port 0 to -1.
  - Peers are configured edge routers only.
  - Graceful restart is off (`rib.go:395`), so a crash withdraws everything.
  - Net effect: a control-plane DoS, with no stale or hijacked routes.
  - Still open: whether BMP-relayed attributes reach the vulnerable parser. That is unverified, and #490 has to answer it.
- **G-05 merged with SEC-02 and SEC-13 (webhook redirects).** Same code: `internal/plugins/notifier/webhook/webhook.go:118`, `:157`, `:161`.
- **G-06 merged with SEC-09 (bootstrap password in the log).** Same line: `cmd/controller/auth.go:200-201`.
- **G-07 merged with SEC-07.** Same code: `internal/extpolicy/server.go:74-79`.
- **G-09 merged with HYG-02 (ASCII gate).**
- **SAFE-07 (lock order) kept at Low and marked latent.** Grok also saw it and declined to file it. The inversion is real (`internal/mitigation/mitigation.go:746-751` documents the one-goroutine assumption), but it cannot deadlock today.
- **Grok-only findings, verified and kept:**
  - G-02: on-wire mitigation skips `Contains` (`mitigation.go:736-745`).
  - G-03: `docs/precedence.md:16` and `:71-73` still say "Not built".
  - G-04: BMP listens on `":"+DefaultPort` (`internal/plugins/ribsource/bmp/bmp.go:116-118`).
  - G-08.
- **Opus findings spot-checked and confirmed:**
  - SAFE-01: `mitigation.go:751` checks outbound only, and there is no inbound hook.
  - SAFE-02: `internal/config/validate.go:277-305` has no `Bits()==0` case.
  - SAFE-04: `internal/announce/announce.go:629` returns before the allowlist check at `:632`.
  - SAFE-06: `internal/plugins/announcer/gobgp/gobgp.go:326-330` calls DeletePath with family only.
  - SEC-02: `os.Expand` at `webhook.go:118`, `:157`.
  - SEC-04: `internal/httpapi/access.go:286-293`, and `troubleshoot.go` never calls `writesEnabled`.
  - SEC-01: `cmd/policy/main.go:36` has no scheme check.
- **Rejected non-findings, carried over from the Opus pass:**
  - Exec plugin added through the editor: refused at `cmd/controller/ui.go:117-135`.
  - Herd `InsecureSkipVerify`: it is pinned.
  - `discord.yml` `pull_request_target`: safe.
  - BMP pre-policy routes: refused.
- **Totals:** 0 findings dropped as false. 1 downgraded (G-01). 5 Grok findings merged into Opus findings as duplicates. 0 duplicates of existing GitHub issues. SAFE-01 is the open inbound counterpart of closed #379.
