Prefixes

Measured prefixes, one page at a time. Traffic now (router) is the learned path; Packeteer recommends is the latest decision's pick. In inject mode the columns are Current and Recommended. Expected gain is how much better the recommended exit scores than the current one. Loss and RTT are those of the current exit, or of the recommended exit when the current one has no successful probe. On router says whether the router advertises the prefix. Details opens the prefix card.
