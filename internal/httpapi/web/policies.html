Policy rules are edited in Settings (#130), together with the flow, VIP, and outage sources. This page does not list them yet.

Maintenance windows

While a window is open, its providers carry no improvements: existing ones are retired and no move may land there. Native routing through the provider is not moved. A window is opened and closed through /api/maintenance (operator role, audited). Times are in your browser's time zone () and are sent as RFC 3339. Configured recurring windows are listed while they are open.
