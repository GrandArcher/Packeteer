First-run wizard

Builds an observe-mode config: it measures and recommends and never announces. The allowlist stays empty. The result opens in the editor below for review; nothing is written until you save.

    Settings

    The form edits providers, probe prefixes, the allowlist, and the knobs that change a decision. The YAML stays beside it: anything the form does not show is kept. Apply copies the form into the YAML and does not write the file. Save runs the same checks as a start and the controller applies the file on restart, or on SIGHUP when only bgp.neighbors changed. Turning inject on still needs the confirmation. Secrets stay in environment variables, not in the form. Needs http.config_editor: true and the admin role.

    Decision

    Providers

    Plus adds an empty row. Minus removes one. Cost is the price per Mbps. Commit is the telemetry binding's commit; the form does not ask for an SNMP community or any other secret.

    Suggestions from BGP

    Next hops on iBGP or BMP that are not configured providers. Accept adds a draft row with the next hop and the AS filled in. Probe source, cost, and commit stay empty. Accepting does not save, does not probe, and does not announce. Exchange LAN hops stay on the exchange view until you add them as peers.

    Prefixes to probe

    The first static source. Other sources stay in the YAML.

    Allowlist

    Prefixes that may be injected. Empty means nothing may be announced.
